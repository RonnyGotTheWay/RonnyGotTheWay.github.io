Project Overview

This project studies whether financial news headlines from 2008 to 2024 contain enough information to explain or predict the next trading day's S&P 500 direction. The workflow aggregates headline text to daily features, adds macro context, compares sentiment methods, and evaluates whether predictions survive realistic backtesting checks.

The final conclusion is deliberately cautious: simple sentiment relationships are weak, classification models stay close to baseline, and backtest gains are sensitive to thresholds, costs, and market regimes.
